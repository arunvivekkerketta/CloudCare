Meet the AI agent that watches for risk before symptoms do

The Patient Health Risk Score agent reads each patient's history — vitals, labs, diagnoses, medication trends — and continuously estimates their risk of chronic conditions like diabetes, cardiac disease and stroke. Doctors see a clear score and a plain-language explanation, not a black box.

  • Recalculates automatically whenever new vitals or lab results come in
  • Surfaces the specific factors driving a patient's score — no guesswork
  • Flags high-risk patients directly on the doctor and admin dashboards
  • Turns reactive checkups into proactive, earlier intervention
