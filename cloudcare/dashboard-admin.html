How the forecast works

Explainable

Every time you record stock used, received, returned or discarded, the assistant adds it to the item's history. With at least five days of recorded use in the last 30 days it fits a simple trend; with less history it uses the 30-day average. Each suggestion shows its confidence so you can judge it.

Status meanings: Reorder now means stock is at or below the reorder level, or will run out within a week. Use stock before expiry means use the oldest stock first. Monitor means stock is close to the line. Healthy means no action is needed today.
